THE TRANSLATION / 02

Nature leaves clues.

The best engineering often starts by paying closer attention to structures that have already solved the problem.

01
SHARK / SURFACE

Riblets align the flow.

Microscopic grooves on shark skin guide water along the body. Here, the idea becomes a surface texture designed to reduce small-scale turbulence.

See it in the model ↗

⌁⌁⌁⌁
OWL / EDGE

Feathers soften the wake.

Owl wing edges break one loud wake into many quieter vortices. A serrated trailing edge gives that strategy a new surface to explore.

See it in the model ↗
03
WIND / SYSTEM

One system, many signals.

Drag and sound are connected. A smoother path through air can mean less energy lost, less wake, and a calmer cabin.
